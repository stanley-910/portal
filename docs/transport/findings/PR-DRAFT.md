# Reliable transport data, global flights and optional hotel rates

Transport searches outside Asia had no candidate airports, several surface routes lacked data or provenance,
and test/fallback results could overstate freshness. This change adds global airport resolution, sourced timetable
coverage and refresh tooling, optional dated TDX and LiteAPI adapters, and preserves usable estimates when access
is missing. Overnight arrivals now drive hotel dates and shared nights. It removes invented China rail fares,
suspended SkyPier service and false live labels on sandbox inventory.

Live inventory is not complete across every brief corridor. Japan rail and several cross-border coach/sea routes
remain source/access-gated; KTMB still publishes an October 17 calendar end. LiteAPI and TDX are contract-tested
but await authenticated production verification. Hostels remain estimates. All access blockers are listed below.

Validation: 789 tests passed (one opt-in live test skipped), lint passed with one existing warning, TypeScript passed,
eight app-route probes passed and the hotel panel was verified in a browser. No bookings were made.
