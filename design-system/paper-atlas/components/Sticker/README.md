# Sticker

A paper cut-out laid on the page: the plane that follows the cursor, or the star pin that marks an origin or destination.

- Provide `shape` (`'plane'` or `'star'`). Optionally set `size` in px, `rotate` in degrees (the plane points north at 0), and `title` when the sticker means something on its own.
- Stickers have no cut border: the ink outline is the edge, and a cast shadow sets them off the page. Pass `altitude` (0 to 1) to move the shadow further off and softer; the plane defaults to 0.5, the star to 0.
- Stickers keep their paper colours in both themes. Never recolour one to match the theme or to show state.
- Use the plane only for the traveller (the cursor while flying, or parked at a destination). Use the star only for trip end points.
- On the globe, draw stickers at 44px (plane) and 26px (star). Smaller copies are fine in lists and on tickets.
